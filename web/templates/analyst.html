⚙️ Documented Tie-Handling & Percentile Logic (Blueprint Section 6)

Standard quintile binning often fails in commercial retail because a massive percentage of customers share identical frequency (e.g., exactly 1 purchase) or zero monetary spend in the observation window. Arbitrary bin splitting would assign two customers with identical order histories different scores.

Recency Tie Rule Customers sharing identical recency days receive the identical minimum percentile tier. Lower recency days earn higher scores (Score 5: top 20% most recent).
Frequency Tie Rule Customers with 0 qualifying orders are explicitly anchored to Score 1. Customers with 1 order share the same fair tier without artificial binning.
Monetary Tie Rule Net spend <= ₹0 is strictly anchored to Score 1. High spenders are bucketed using smooth cumulative percentile thresholds.
